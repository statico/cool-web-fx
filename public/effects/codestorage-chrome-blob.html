Chrome Blob

A liquid-chrome blob that wobbles and slowly turns inside a ring of rotating mono text. Drag it to spin it, and it hums while you do.

How it works

This ports the 3D "metaball" in the hero of code.storage. It is not a real metaball field. It is a single SphereGeometry(1.4, 64, 64) with the uv attribute deleted and the vertices merged, so the seam stays closed when it is pushed around.

Every frame, the CPU moves each vertex out along its own direction from the centre by three octaves of a cheap trig noise, n(p, t) = sin(2x + t)·cos(3y + 0.5t)·sin(1.5z + 0.8t). The sum is 0.30·n(0.5p, 0.5t) + 0.15·n(1.2p, 0.8t) + 0.08·n(2p, 1.2t). Then computeVertexNormals() rebuilds the shading. The big, slow octave gives the lumpy silhouette, and the two finer ones make the surface ripple.

The look is almost all material. It is a MeshPhysicalMaterial at metalness 0.98, roughness 0.01, clearcoat 1, transmission 0.1 and ior 2.4, lit mostly by an environment map with envMapIntensity 2. Roughness, metalness, env intensity and reflectivity each drift on their own slow sine, so the chrome seems to breathe. The mesh turns +0.005 rad on y and +0.002 rad on x each frame, and grows in from scale 0.01 on load. OrbitControls let you drag it around (no zoom or pan). While you drag, a small Web Audio hum plays: a triangle and a sine at 270/540 Hz that drop to 144/288 Hz, through a lowpass, with 6 Hz vibrato.

Behind it is plain SVG: two dashed rings, a solid dot with a "+", and a text ring that turns once every 60s.

The site uses Poly Haven's *empty_warehouse_01* HDR for reflections, which reads too white on a black page. This port loads Poly Haven's darker *studio_small_03* HDRI (CC0, 1k, about 1.6MB) from their CDN with RGBELoader, set as scene.environment with equirectangular reflection mapping. The site's scroll morph into a voxel shape is not ported. This port is also recoloured to dark mode (white on black), and the rings are centred on the blob. Three.js is pinned and loaded from a CDN via an importmap.
